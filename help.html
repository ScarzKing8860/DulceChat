Common Issues

  • Camera not starting: allow browser camera permission and refresh the page.
  • Chat not connecting: double-check the WS URL and ensure the server is online.
  • Tips not appearing: refresh the page and confirm local storage is allowed.

Quick Tips

Use a well-lit room, keep your background simple, and test your webcam before going live.
