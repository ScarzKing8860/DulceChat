Support center

Good questions welcome.

A few quick answers for finding rooms, using tokens, and keeping the community kind.

How do tokens work?

Tokens are used for digital gifts. Choose a gift in a live room, confirm the amount, and the creator receives the support in their studio balance.

How do I enter a room?

Open Discover, choose a live room, and select the room card. You can browse previews before joining the conversation.
